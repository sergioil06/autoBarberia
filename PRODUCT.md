## Users

- **Primary:** men aged 18–35 in Gijón who want a current cut (fades, urban styles). Most arrive on a phone, often from Instagram, and decide fast: does this place cut the way I want, how much, when can I go.
- **Secondary:** men 35+, regulars who want a reliable classic cut and beard work, and mostly need the hours, address and a way to get an appointment.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Llano Peluqueros, a men's barbershop in Gijón. It exists to turn visits into appointments: show the work, the services and prices, the team, and where and when the shop is open, then get the visitor to book. Success is booked appointments and visitors who choose Llano over nearby competitors.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Llano Peluqueros**.
- Binding from the owner: modern, must not look AI-generated, must compare favorably with competitors.
- Banned clichés (owner brief): striped barber pole, moustache icons, crossed-scissors logo, generic black+gold, default Playfair Display, fake wood or leather textures, generic stock photos. A cliché may only appear if deliberately reinterpreted.
- Copy in Spanish, close and direct, no filler.
- **Standing preference (chosen in the direction round):** the category standard played straight: a modern barbershop site — dark ground, large photography, condensed display type, no governing metaphor — executed at full craft so it beats local competitors on finish, not on concept. Local Gijón metaphors (seafront, tide table, sidrería) were offered and declined.

## Product Principles

1. **Booking is the point.** Every screen moves the visitor one step closer to an appointment; the booking action is always within reach on mobile.
2. **Show the work, not adjectives.** Real cuts and real people persuade; claims without evidence do not ship.
3. **Answer the three questions fast:** how do they cut, how much, when can I go.
4. **Local and specific.** This is a shop on Calle Velázquez in Gijón, not a generic barbershop anywhere.
5. **Honest placeholders over invented content.**
